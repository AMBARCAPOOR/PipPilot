Quick Guide
Quick Calculator Mode: choose Manual TP Prices when you already know entry, SL, and TP1/TP2/TP3.
Strategy Mode: choose Auto R Targets when you want the tool to create TP levels from your stop distance.
Beginner rule: risk 1% or less until you have a proven sample size.
USD/JPY: pip value changes with price. Enter the current USD/JPY rate before calculating.
XAU/USD: gold margin and pip math vary heavily by broker. Verify contract specs before trading.
Hard stop: if the tool says NO TRADE because of risk limits, reduce size, widen account buffer, or skip the trade.
New: Gugrji mode grades the trade before you enter, prop presets auto-fill common challenge rules, and Replay mode lets you review logged setups later.
